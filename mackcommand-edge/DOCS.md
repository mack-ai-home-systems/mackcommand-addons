# MackCommand

The home, in one place — rooms, climate, routines, cameras, security and access, on a
Home Assistant hub. Home Assistant is the engine; MackCommand is the experience.

## Access

- Clients reach MackCommand at their own address and sign in with a MackCommand account.
- Home Assistant itself is never exposed to the household.

## Support

Mack AI Home Systems — support contact is set in the add-on configuration.
